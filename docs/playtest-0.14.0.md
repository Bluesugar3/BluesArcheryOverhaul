# Play-test 0.14.0 — three side orbs and the piercing line

Restart the server and the game first; both must be on 0.14.0. All seven tests can be done alone.

Shortcut: double-click `tools\Test Build Menu.cmd` on the PC running the server. Each button lends you exactly the skills for that test (the server picks it up within 2 seconds, nothing is saved). Lent skills work in play but do not show as owned in the shrine, so use "Clear" for test 1 and for buying Shatter in test 7.

Know before you start: a Quickdraw 4 line now passes through creatures, so the arrow no longer stays in the creature you hit. It ends in whatever is behind it. Without Quiver Gather you will lose arrows faster.

## 1. The shrine shows the new tree
- **Skills:** Any.
- **Do:** Open the archery tree and look at the third orb of each line.
- **You should see:** One new orb beside each: Auto Dock beside Quickdraw 3, Shatter beside Charge Shot 3, Ricochet beside Burst. Each has its own icon. Hold each one and read the tablet. Brace and Overcharge are gone.
- **Wrong if:** A new orb is missing, sits on top of another orb, shares an icon, or the tablet says nothing about it.

## 2. Auto Dock nocks the next arrow
- **Skills:** Quickdraw 1, 2, 3 and Auto Dock. Wear a quiver with several arrows.
- **Do:** Nock an arrow by hand and shoot. Keep holding the bow and do not touch the string. Then shoot five in a row without reaching for the quiver.
- **You should see:** About a third of a second after each shot, the next arrow leaves the quiver and sits on the string, ready to pull. Each arrow flies normally.
- **Wrong if:** No arrow appears, the arrow hits your own bow or body when you shoot it, an arrow appears from nowhere with an empty quiver, or arrows land on the ground.

## 3. Auto Dock and holstering
- **Skills:** As test 2.
- **Do:** Shoot once, let the next arrow nock itself, then put the bow away on your back without shooting. Check the quiver.
- **You should see:** The arrow that was on the string goes back into the quiver a moment later.
- **Wrong if:** The arrow drops on the ground and stays there.

## 4. Ricochet: ghost arrows skip off the ground
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Two-Shot, Three-Shot, Burst and Ricochet (multi-arrow as your main path).
- **Do:** Hold a full volley and shoot it at the ground about 15 metres ahead at a shallow angle. Then shoot one almost straight down at your feet.
- **You should see:** On the shallow shot the ghost arrows hit the ground with a white spark and a click, then carry on forward, a little slower and slightly upward. On the steep shot they just land.
- **Wrong if:** Nothing skips, they bounce backwards or straight up, or they skip more than once.

## 5. Ricochet: a hit jumps to another creature
- **Skills:** As test 4.
- **Do:** Find two creatures within a few metres of each other. Shoot a full volley at one of them.
- **You should see:** A thin arc jumps from the creature you hit to the other one, with a small spark, and the second creature takes damage.
- **Wrong if:** No arc, the second creature takes nothing, the arc returns to the first creature, or a lone creature gets an arc to nowhere.

## 6. Quickdraw 4 pierces, Charge Shot 3 does not
- **Skills:** Sell everything. First Quickdraw 1 to 4. Then sell and buy Charge Shot 1 to 3.
- **Do:** As a quick archer, line up two or three creatures and fire the instant line shot through them. As a charge archer, fire a full three-stage charged arrow at the same line-up.
- **You should see:** The line passes through every creature with a small flash at each and ends at the ground or wall behind; the first takes the most damage, the ones behind take less. The charged arrow stops in the first creature.
- **Wrong if:** The line stops at the first creature, only the first is hurt, or the charged arrow still goes through.

## 7. Shatter throws splinters behind the target
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1 to 3 and Shatter (Charge Shot as your main path). To buy Shatter, hold it, then charge an arrow until the thump and shoot.
- **Do:** Line up two creatures, one a few metres behind the other. Charge until at least the first thump and hit the front one.
- **You should see:** A shattering sound and a burst of small orange streaks flying on from the first creature; the one behind takes a little damage.
- **Wrong if:** No splinters, the splinters hurt the creature you hit, a plain uncharged arrow shatters, or Shatter cannot be bought after the try-out shot.
