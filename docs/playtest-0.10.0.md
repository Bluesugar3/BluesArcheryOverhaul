# Play-test 0.10.0 — one main path per archer

Restart the server and the game first. Sell every archery orb before test 1.

Your main path is the line you own the most orbs in. On a tie, the one that was main first stays main. Nothing at the shrine shows it yet, so follow the buying order in each test exactly. The extra arrows still use their old looks in this version: needles look like the faint extra arrow, heavy arrows look like gold ghost arrows.

## 1. A quick archer has only the quick shot
- **Skills:** Quickdraw 1, 2 and 3. Nothing else.
- **Do:** Shoot once by releasing the instant the gold ring closes. Then shoot once after holding for 3 seconds.
- **You should see:** The instant shot is a quick shot, as before. The held shot is a plain arrow: no strands, no thump, no ghost arrows.
- **Wrong if:** The held shot charges or grows ghost arrows.

## 2. Two-Shot gives a quick archer a needle
- **Skills:** Keep Quickdraw 1 to 3. Buy Two-Shot.
- **Do:** Fire three quick shots at a target. Then hold one shot for 2 seconds and release.
- **You should see:** Each quick shot fires one faint extra arrow beside it, right away. The held shot still has no ghost arrows.
- **Wrong if:** A quick shot has no extra arrow, or holding gives you the five ghosts.

## 3. A tie keeps your main path, one more orb flips it
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Buy Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3 first. Then buy Quickdraw 1, 2 and 3.
- **Do:** Release instantly once. Then hold a full charge and release.
- **You should see:** No gold ring and no quick shot: you are still a charge archer. The charge works, with three strands and three thumps.
- **Wrong if:** The gold ring appears or the instant shot is a quick shot.

## 4. Quickdraw orbs make a charge archer faster
- **Skills:** The build from test 3 (Charge Shot 1 to 3, then Quickdraw 1 to 3).
- **Do:** Hold a full charge and count the seconds to the third thump. Sell the three Quickdraw orbs and do it again.
- **You should see:** With the Quickdraw orbs the third thump comes at about 1.6 seconds. Without them it comes at about 2.5 seconds.
- **Wrong if:** Both take the same time.

## 5. Two-Shot gives a charge archer heavy arrows
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1 to 3, then buy Two-Shot.
- **Do:** Hold a full charge and release at a target.
- **You should see:** At the first thump, two gold arrows slide out beside your arrow. They fly with it when you release. There is no five-ghost volley.
- **Wrong if:** No gold arrows appear, or you get the five ghosts.

## 6. Quickdraw orbs make a multi-arrow archer faster and tighter
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Buy Two-Shot and Three-Shot first. Then buy Quickdraw 1.
- **Do:** Pull, wait for the ghost arrows, release. Then sell Quickdraw 1 and do it again.
- **You should see:** With Quickdraw 1 the ghosts appear sooner and sit a little closer together. Releasing instantly never gives a quick shot.
- **Wrong if:** There is no difference, or the gold ring appears.

## 7. Charge Shot orbs make a multi-arrow archer's ghosts gold
- **Skills:** Two-Shot and Three-Shot first, then buy Charge Shot 1.
- **Do:** Pull, wait for the ghost arrows, release at a Turabada.
- **You should see:** The ghost arrows are gold instead of blue, and the gold ring at the Turabada's feet fills a little when they hit. Holding longer gives no strands and no thump.
- **Wrong if:** The ghosts stay blue, or the shot charges like a Charge Shot.
