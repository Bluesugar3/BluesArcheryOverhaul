# Play-test 0.11.0 — the new looks

Restart the server and the game first. Nothing about how shots work changed in this version, only what you see, hear and feel.

## 1. Charge strands sit on the arrow and finish at the head
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3. Nothing else.
- **Do:** Pull fully and hold for 3 seconds. Look at the arrow from the side.
- **You should see:** Three strands hugging the shaft, thin at both ends, closing onto the arrowhead. Each thump sends a quick pulse along its strand and leaves one small bright knot behind the head: one, two, then three. At the third thump the arrowhead itself glows and stays lit.
- **Wrong if:** The strands float away from the shaft, sink inside it, or just stop short of the head.

## 2. The arrow gets brighter at each thump
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3.
- **Do:** Pull fully and watch the strands through all three thumps.
- **You should see:** At each thump the strands get visibly brighter and a little thicker.
- **Wrong if:** You cannot tell stage 1 from stage 3 by brightness.

## 3. The Holy Arrow wind-up
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3, then Holy Arrow.
- **Do:** Pull fully and hold until Holy Arrow is ready, then release.
- **You should see:** After the third thump the strands slowly turn from orange to pale gold. Small bright motes drift from your hand toward the head, faster and faster. Near the end the strands wind tight into a cord at the head. When it is ready, the cord pulses and a soft ring breathes around the arrowhead. Your bow hand ticks as motes arrive.
- **Wrong if:** Nothing changes between the third thump and the beam, or the ticking in your hand is annoying.

## 4. A quick archer's needles
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Quickdraw 1, 2 and 3 first, then Two-Shot and Three-Shot.
- **Do:** Fire a few quick shots at a wall or target.
- **You should see:** Two thin gold streaks leave with each quick shot, each with a hair-thin trail that fades after it lands, and two sharp "tk" sounds. They do not look like arrows.
- **Wrong if:** The extras still look like faint ghost arrows, or there is no "tk".

## 5. A charge archer's heavy arrows
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3 first, then Two-Shot and Three-Shot.
- **Do:** Pull fully, hold through all three thumps, release at a target.
- **You should see:** At the first thump, thick orange-gold arrows slide out beside yours in pairs, two pairs in total, each wrapped in one turn of a strand. They brighten with the later thumps, fly with ember trails, and land with a small charge burst.
- **Wrong if:** They look like the thin fanned ghost arrows, or all four appear at once.

## 6. A multi-arrow archer's ghosts stay thin
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Two-Shot and Three-Shot first, then Charge Shot 1.
- **Do:** Pull, wait for all the ghost arrows, release. If you own Burst, hold for the click and release once more.
- **You should see:** The ghost arrows are thin, fanned and gold. Burst's pellets are blue, not gold.
- **Wrong if:** The ghosts look like the thick heavy arrows from test 5, or the pellets are gold.
