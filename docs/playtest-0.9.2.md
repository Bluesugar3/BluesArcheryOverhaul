# Play-test 0.9.2 — Charge Shot, Quickdraw and multi-arrow

Restart the server and the game first. Sell every archery orb before test 1.

## 1. One Charge Shot orb
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1 only.
- **Do:** Pull the bow fully and hold for 2 seconds.
- **You should see:** An orange strand winds around the outside of the arrow from your hand to the head. At about 1 second it thumps, brightens, and the whole arrow glows like the original game.
- **Wrong if:** The strand is hidden inside the arrow, or the arrow never glows.

## 2. Three orbs, three steps
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3.
- **Do:** Pull fully and hold for 3 seconds.
- **You should see:** Three strands wind on one after another, with a thump at about 1 s, 1.75 s and 2.5 s. Each thump is heavier than the last.
- **Wrong if:** All three appear at once, or there is only one thump.

## 3. Letting go early is weaker
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3.
- **Do:** Shoot a Turabada once right after the first thump, then once after the third.
- **You should see:** The late shot fills the gold ring at its feet more than the early one.
- **Wrong if:** Both shots fill the ring by the same amount.

## 4. Quickdraw with Two-Shot
- **Skills:** Quickdraw 1 to 3 and Two-Shot.
- **Do:** Pull and release at three speeds: instantly, after a short beat (about half a second), and after 1 second.
- **You should see:** Instantly: a quick shot with one faint extra arrow. Short beat and 1 second: five ghost arrows.
- **Wrong if:** The short-beat shot gives neither a quick shot nor five ghosts.

## 5. Holy Arrow
- **Skills:** Charge Shot 1 to 3 and Holy Arrow.
- **Do:** Pull fully and hold until the Holy Arrow is ready, then release.
- **You should see:** Three strands and three thumps, the arrow glowing throughout, the strands squeezing toward the head near the end, then the beam.
- **Wrong if:** The arrow is not glowing while you wait.
