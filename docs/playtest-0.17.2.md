# Play-test 0.17.2 — homing arrows that land, the new tree, one gatherer orb

Server and every player's game must be on 0.17.2 (send your friends the new file). This build includes the finished homing looks from 0.17.1, which you have not seen yet either.

Nothing here has been run in the game. The test numbers come from flat planes and boxes, not real creatures and terrain. This build has not finished its second-opinion check: if the game or server closes, or a bow stops working, stop and tell me.

The test menu has two changed buttons: "Spawn 4 Turabadas (side by side)" and "Spawn 3 moving Turabadas".

## 1. Do the arrows land now
- **Skills:** Press "Homing".
- **Do:** Press "Spawn 3 Turabadas". Lock the nearest one from about 10 metres and release. Do it again from about 25 and about 50 metres.
- **You should see:** Green arrows spread a little, then curve in from above and the sides and land on the creature. Nearly all of them hit at every distance.
- **Wrong if:** Arrows dive into the ground in front of the creature, or clearly more than a handful miss.

## 2. The lock rings, and four locks
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Press "Remove test enemies", then "Spawn 4 Turabadas (side by side)". Lock each in turn, walking a few steps sideways while you hold. Release.
- **You should see:** Rings close round each body like a cage, from any side, in about half a second. The first lock is brighter with one extra ring. The volley splits four ways and every creature takes hits.
- **Wrong if:** A ring is a flat circle facing you, an earlier cage vanishes while you lock the next, or all the arrows go to one creature.

## 3. A creature that dies mid-volley
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Shoot one Turabada with plain arrows until it is nearly dead. Lock it and a healthy one, then release.
- **You should see:** When the hurt one dies, the arrows still in the air bend toward the other and hit it.
- **Wrong if:** The leftover arrows fly on for seconds, or drop into the ground by the dead one.

## 4. A creature that moves
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Press "Remove test enemies", then "Spawn 3 moving Turabadas". Lock one while it walks and release. These ones will come for you.
- **You should see:** The cage stays on it as it moves, and the arrows follow it and land on it.
- **Wrong if:** The arrows land where it stood when you let go, or the cage lags behind it.

## 5. The hidden arrow at the bow
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Release a locked volley, then grab the string again at once and keep your hand by the bow while the arrows fly.
- **You should see:** Nothing odd at the bow. The next arrow nocks normally. Your real arrow appears stuck in the creature with a green flash.
- **Wrong if:** The bow twitches or is pushed, your hand closes on something invisible, or the next arrow will not nock.

## 6. The tree and the one gatherer orb
- **Skills:** Any.
- **Do:** Open the archery tree at the shrine. Then take an arrow from your quiver, shoot it, and wait 15 seconds.
- **You should see:** An even grid: Charge Shot straight up the middle, Quickdraw on the left and the multi-arrow line on the right, each stepping out one column after its second orb. The three top orbs sit side by side. One gatherer orb alone at the bottom left, with the quiver icon. If you owned Quiver Gather, you have one skill point back. The arrow returns to your quiver.
- **Wrong if:** Two orbs touch, a line runs through an orb, there are two gatherer orbs, the point did not come back, or the arrow stays where it landed.

## 7. A friend watching, and an ordinary volley
- **Skills:** You as in test 1; your friend anything.
- **Do:** Lock and shoot a Turabada about 20 metres away while your friend watches it. Then shoot an ordinary volley at a wall with no lock.
- **You should see (the watcher):** Green arrows curve in and land, and your real arrow appears stuck in the creature. The ordinary volley looks exactly as it did before: normal colours, your real arrow flying.
- **Wrong if:** The watcher sees the real arrow appear somewhere else first, or the ordinary volley is green or curves.

## If you are near a cave
Lock something inside a low tunnel and release. The arrows should stay under the ceiling, fanning sideways instead of over the top. Tell me if they hit the roof.

## Known rough edges
- If you were already holding the most skill points the game lets you bank, the point you get back for Quiver Gather puts you over that limit, and archery experience stops until you spend a point.
- The shrine's own description of the gatherer orb is the game's text and still only mentions the pouch. The stone tablet says both.
- If the server disagrees with your lock, the green arrows vanish and your real arrow flies as an ordinary shot.
