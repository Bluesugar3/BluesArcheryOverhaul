# Play-test 0.17.1 — the homing arrow's finished look

Server and every player's game must be on 0.17.1 (send your friends the new file: the lock time changed, and that is shared). Use the test menu's "Homing" button for every test.

This build changes looks and the lock time only. Arrows that hit the ground or miss are NOT fixed yet: that is the next build.

None of this has been seen in the game. Sizes, brightness and the green tint are first guesses and every one is a live setting.

## 1. The cage and the quicker lock
- **Skills:** Press "Homing".
- **Do:** Press "Spawn 3 Turabadas". Draw fully, rest your aim on the nearest one, and walk a quarter circle round it while holding.
- **You should see:** Three green arcs, wide and open, tighten round its body and close in about half a second, then snap shut with a flash, a sound and a pulse in your bow hand. From the side it is still a cage round the body.
- **Wrong if:** It is a flat ring facing you, it shows through a rock, or it is too thin to see from 40 metres.

## 2. Four locks, the first one stands out
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Press "Remove test enemies", then "Spawn 4 Turabadas". Lock each in turn.
- **You should see:** Four cages. The first has thicker, brighter arcs and one extra, larger ring tumbling round it. The other three are dimmer.
- **Wrong if:** You cannot tell which was first, or an earlier cage vanishes while you lock the next.

## 3. The bow tells you
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Lock a Turabada, keep holding, swing the bow slowly away from it and back.
- **You should see:** The spectral arrows on the bow turn green in a quick sweep and their heads lean toward the creature. Pointing far away they go back to their own colour; green again when you come back.
- **Wrong if:** The bow is green with no lock, or stays green when you point far away.

## 4. Flight and arrival
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Lock a Turabada about 25 metres away, aim a hand-width to one side, release.
- **You should see:** Bigger green arrows with long tapering trails curve in smoothly, five brighter ones in the middle. Where they land: a green burst, a thump in your bow hand, the cage thrown outward, and your real arrow appearing in a green flash.
- **Wrong if:** Trails show corners or jump sideways, any arrow is gold or blue, the real arrow pops in, or the cage stays on the creature after the hit.

## 5. A lock lost
- **Skills:** As test 1.
- **Do:** Lock a Turabada, then swing the bow well away for half a second.
- **You should see:** The cage springs open and fades quickly with a sound. The bow goes back to its own colour.
- **Wrong if:** The cage just vanishes, or lingers.

## 6. A friend watching, and an ordinary volley
- **Skills:** You as in test 1; your friend anything.
- **Do:** Lock and shoot a Turabada about 20 metres away while your friend looks at it. Then shoot an ordinary volley at a wall with no lock.
- **You should see (the watcher):** No cage while you hold. Green arrows curve in, and a cage flashes outward on the creature as they land. The ordinary volley looks exactly as it did before.
- **Wrong if:** The watcher sees the lock forming, or the ordinary volley is green, bigger or has long trails.

## One thing to watch at the bow
While a locked volley is in the air your real arrow is hidden. Tell me if the bow twitches, if you can feel or grab something invisible at the string, or if the next arrow will not nock.
