# Play-test 0.13.0 — fixes from your last test

Restart the server and the game first. Tests 1 to 5 you can do alone; tests 6 and 7 need your friend.

## 1. The tablet sits in the stone fork and rests when unused
- **Skills:** Any.
- **Do:** Walk up to the archery shrine without opening the tree and look at the four-pronged stone fork to the right of it. Then open the tree, wait, and close it.
- **You should see:** Before you open the tree, a small folded slab resting in the fork with crystals tucked around it and a dim gem. When you open the tree it rises and unfolds over about a second and a half and turns to face you. When you close the tree it sinks back and turns to its resting direction.
- **Wrong if:** It is not in the fork, it hangs in the air or sits inside stone, or it stays up after you close the tree.

## 2. The lettering behaves like paint on stone
- **Skills:** Any.
- **Do:** With the tree open, pass your hand and your bow in front of the tablet. Then walk behind it.
- **You should see:** Your hand and bow cover the lettering. From behind you see plain stone with no mirrored writing.
- **Wrong if:** The lettering shows through anything, is missing, or looks like solid blocks instead of letters.

## 3. Heavy arrows sit close and even
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Charge Shot 1, 2 and 3 first, then Two-Shot. Repeat after buying Three-Shot, then Burst.
- **Do:** Charge fully and look at the arrows beside yours, then shoot a wall from about 10 metres.
- **You should see:** With Two-Shot, one heavy arrow on each side. With Three-Shot too, four in a plus: left, right, above, below. With Burst too, six in a ring. All sit close to your arrow and land in a tight group.
- **Wrong if:** Any heavy arrow sits far off, the shape leans to one side, or the shape turns when you let go.

## 4. Tighter multi-arrow, compressing spiral, dimmer charge
- **Skills:** First Two-Shot and Three-Shot only. Then sell and buy Charge Shot 1 to 3 and Holy Arrow.
- **Do:** As a multi-arrow archer, shoot a full volley at a wall. As a charge archer, hold a full charge through to Holy Arrow and release.
- **You should see:** The volley lands in a noticeably smaller group than before. On the charge, after the third thump the spiral slides forward and packs tightly into the front of the arrow before Holy Arrow is ready. The charged arrow glows less harshly than last time.
- **Wrong if:** The volley is as wide as before, the spiral stays spread along the whole arrow, or the glow is still too bright (tell me; it is one setting).

## 5. Charge Shot orbs show on a quick archer's shots
- **Skills:** Sell everything. Quickdraw 1, 2 and 3 first, then Charge Shot 1 and 2.
- **Do:** Pull and watch the arrowhead as the gold ring closes, fire a quick shot at a creature, then sell both Charge Shot orbs and fire again.
- **You should see:** With the Charge Shot orbs: a small orange ember on the arrowhead while the ring closes, a thin orange trail behind the arrow, a small orange burst where it hits the creature, a click, and the gold ring at the creature's feet filling a little. Without them: none of that.
- **Wrong if:** Nothing changes when you own the Charge Shot orbs.

## 6. Your friend's Holy Arrow (two players)
- **Skills:** Your friend has Charge Shot 1 to 3 and Holy Arrow. You watch from the side.
- **Do:** Your friend fires a Holy Arrow at the ground about 20 metres away, then one into the sky. Then swap.
- **You should see:** For the ground shot, a gold bolt from their bow to the landing spot, then the blast and the marks on creatures caught in it. For the sky shot, nothing left hanging at their bow.
- **Wrong if:** You see no bolt or no blast, or a gold line stays stuck under their bow.

## 7. The tablet for a bystander, and PvP (two players)
- **Skills:** Any archery orbs on both of you.
- **Do:** Your friend opens the tree while you stand beside the shrine. Then they close it. Afterwards, shoot each other once with a plain arrow.
- **You should see:** The tablet rises for you too, faces your friend, and reads "<their name>'s archery build" with their build. It sinks when they close the tree. The plain arrow hurts.
- **Wrong if:** The tablet stays down for you, faces away so you see blank stone, or the arrow does no damage.
